Chinese battery brand Sunwoda has revealed a system charging 10→97% SOC in under 10 minutes at room temperature, adding 100 km of range per minute. It joins CATL, BYD, and SVOLT in the flash-charging race, signalling sub-10-minute charging is becoming table stakes for Chinese EV supply chains.
