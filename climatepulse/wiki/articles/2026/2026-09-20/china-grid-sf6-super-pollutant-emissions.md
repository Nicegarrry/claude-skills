Inside Climate News estimates China's state-owned grid operators could recover an additional 1,400 t of SF6 per year for ~$6M, barely 0.01% of annual grid capex, yet most is currently vented at end of equipment life. SF6 concentration hit its largest annual jump since 1998 last year, with China responsible for more than half of global emissions of the 24,300× CO2-equivalent gas.
