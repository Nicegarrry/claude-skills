Tesla has announced the Tesla Semi is now entering volume production at its Nevada factory, seven years after its original 2019 target. The milestone matters for commercial fleet decarbonisation timelines: a credible high-volume supply source for Class 8 EVs reduces the barrier for logistics operators committing to electrification.
