Snowy Hydro has begun laying concrete foundations for the massive turbine cavern approximately one kilometre underground at the Snowy 2.0 pumped hydro project. The milestone confirms the project has advanced past its most contested phase and is now in active delivery, with first power generation targeted by late 2027.
