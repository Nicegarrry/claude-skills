A Chinese province has introduced rules allowing grid-side batteries to bid directly into the spot electricity market, the first such mechanism in China. Given China's dominant share of global BESS deployment, a successful pilot could catalyse merchant-model revenue stacks nationally and reshape project economics for developers operating there.
