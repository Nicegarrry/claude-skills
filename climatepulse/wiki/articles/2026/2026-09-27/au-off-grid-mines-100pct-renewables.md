Australian off-grid mining sites — typically among the highest-cost electricity consumers — are leading the nation's industrial transition, with several now running near-100% on solar, wind, and battery combinations. The business case is driven by diesel displacement economics rather than policy, making these projects replicable across remote resources globally.
