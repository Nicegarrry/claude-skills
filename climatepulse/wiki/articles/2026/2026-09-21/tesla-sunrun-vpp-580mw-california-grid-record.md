Tesla and Sunrun pooled 140,000+ home batteries to dispatch a record 580 MW onto California's grid during the September 9 heat wave — the largest residential VPP event ever recorded. The three-hour evening dispatch validated the grid services revenue thesis for distributed storage at a scale that is now material to grid operators.
