The Texas PUC backed away from its March data-centre interconnection proposals, eliminating a non-refundable fee, halving financial security requirements, and extending energisation milestones. The softened rules reduce friction for AI/hyperscale data-centre load growth in ERCOT, which is a key tailwind for new dispatchable generation and storage investment in Texas.
