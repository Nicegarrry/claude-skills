Ohio's Power Siting Board approved Oak Run Solar, an 800 MW solar + 300 MW battery project in Madison County — Ohio's largest — after years of legal and regulatory delay. The approval removes a high-profile example of US permitting risk and strengthens the investment case for co-located solar+storage in Midcontinent markets.
