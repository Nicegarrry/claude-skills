North Carolina's utilities commission rejected Duke Energy's proposed 255 MW gas peaking plant — a rare denial from a panel that typically defers to the state's dominant utility. The ruling suggests even Southeast US regulators are beginning to weigh gas lock-in risk, raising the hurdle for new gas generation capital allocation.
