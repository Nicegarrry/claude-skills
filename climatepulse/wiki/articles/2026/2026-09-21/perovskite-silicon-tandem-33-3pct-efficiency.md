Chinese researchers achieved 33.3% efficiency (33.0% certified) in a perovskite-silicon tandem cell fabricated under ambient conditions using a novel PNCC polymer hole-transport layer. The ability to manufacture at this efficiency outside a cleanroom is the commercialisation unlock the tandem cell market has been waiting for.
