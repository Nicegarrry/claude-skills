New York regulators approved the $3.3B, 90-mile PROPEL NY transmission line to improve downstate power delivery by 2030, developed by New York Transco and NYPA. The approval is the largest US transmission infrastructure decision in recent months and validates the investment thesis for grid buildout needed to absorb offshore wind.
