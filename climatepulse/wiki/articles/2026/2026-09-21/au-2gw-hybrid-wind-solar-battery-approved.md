Australia's EPBC approved a 2 GW wind, solar and battery hybrid project targeting Australia's largest isolated grid (WA SWIS), with conditions requiring taller turbines to protect endangered black cockatoos. The scale and isolation of the project make it a template for hybrid remote-grid renewables projects elsewhere in WA and globally.
