Australia's Intergenerational Report projects coal and gas output could shrink ~70% by 2050 under a Paris-aligned path, with electrification rising to fill the gap. Climate impact uncertainty remains high but the government's own 40-year numbers now frame the risk for institutional investors exposed to fossil fuel assets.
