VicGrid has released its preferred route for the 60 km transmission line connecting offshore wind to the main grid at the Latrobe Valley — the critical enabler for Victoria's offshore wind pipeline. The route decision de-risks transmission for developers seeking FID and signals the government is advancing hard infrastructure ahead of its 2032 renewables target.
