A federal judge (Trump-appointed) ruled the EPA illegally cancelled the $7B Solar for All program, finding the One Big Beautiful Bill Act did not authorise its termination. The ruling reinstates one of the largest federal solar programmes and signals that executive energy rollbacks face meaningful judicial checks — a material risk repricing for solar project finance in affected states.
