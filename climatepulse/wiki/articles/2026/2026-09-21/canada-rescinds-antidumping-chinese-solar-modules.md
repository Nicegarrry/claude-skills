Canada's trade tribunal terminated its five-year review of anti-dumping/countervailing duties on Chinese solar modules and laminates, rescinding the order after finding no domestic-producer support. The ruling lowers Canadian solar procurement costs materially and signals divergence from US trade policy on Chinese panels.
