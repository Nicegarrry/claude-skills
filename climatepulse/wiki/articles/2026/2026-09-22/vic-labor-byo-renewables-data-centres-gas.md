Victoria's Labor government confirmed it backs the federal requirement that new data centres source dedicated renewable energy rather than gas, diverging sharply from the Coalition's gas-first position. The alignment creates a clearer policy pathway for renewable energy procurement agreements tied to hyperscaler demand.
